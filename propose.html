Leave Your Mark on Hours Lived

Anyone can propose a phrase. It's free and always will be: your words can become part of someone else's moment of introspection.

Your proposal

Every proposal is read with care. Accepted phrases arrive with upcoming updates, credited as you chose. Not every proposal can be included; if one doesn't quite fit, I may reach out to find an alternative together.

Quote types and attribution

To maintain transparency and credit original works, suggestions must fit into one of these modalities:

Direct Quote

A verbatim quote from a book, movie, game, or anime with precise character/author credit and source title.

Inspired Thought

A reflection in your own words inspired by the themes of a particular work, attributed to you with reference to the original work.

Original Reflection

A 100% original thought born from your own life experiences, credited to your nickname or anonymously.

Moderation guidelines

  • Tone: Calm, acceptance, introspection, or comfort.
  • No promotions or personal data: No links, social media handles, advertising, or contact information.
  • Absolute respect: Any offensive, discriminatory, violent, or disparaging content will be rejected.
  • Localization adjustments: Texts may receive minor adjustments to facilitate translation.
