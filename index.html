Features & Privacy

Your memories, 100% secure

Your thoughts and daily entries are saved securely only on your phone's storage with local military-grade encryption. Nothing is uploaded to servers or the cloud.

Absolute privacy, no tracking

The app does not contain ads, does not track your behavior, and does not gather any analytics data. It is a completely private and intimate space.

Voice Notes & Microphone

Record audio reflections voluntarily. The microphone is only active when you decide to record, and audio files are stored exclusively on your device with local encryption.

Clean Interface & Markdown

Focus on what matters with a minimal distraction-free editor supporting markdown and secure daily journaling.

Nostalgic Custom Themes

Customize your experience with color schemes inspired by classic visual novels and anime classics, or create your own custom themes.

Cross-Platform Desktop (Alpha)

Bring your memory sanctuary to your desktop (Windows & Linux). Experience the same offline privacy, immersive design, and full .lifelogs backup compatibility.

Privacy Policy & Microphone Usage

1. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Hours Lived requests access to your microphone solely when you deliberately choose to record a voice note attached to personal memories. The microphone is never activated in the background or without your explicit command.

2. Strictly Local Storage & Zero Transmission

All voice recordings are processed and stored exclusively within your device's private encrypted storage (and inside your encrypted backup files). No audio recordings are ever transmitted to cloud servers, shared with third parties, or used for voice recognition profiling.

3. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain complete control over your data: you can revoke microphone access at any time through system settings and permanently delete any voice recording by deleting the corresponding memory inside the app.

About the Project

Hours Lived is a 100% offline-first introspective memory sanctuary designed to record lived time and treasure daily reflections in absolute privacy.

Creative Inspiration

The overall concept, aesthetics, and philosophy of Hours Lived are primarily inspired by "Plastic Memories". The premise of recording precious memories and being fully aware of the passage of our finite time is the backbone of the project.

"We spend so much time dreading the expiration date of our hearts, that we forget we are the ones who give meaning to the seconds we have left."

— Plastic Memories

The soundtracks and musical compositions from these works were the soundscape accompanying every line of code, design, and reflection. You can listen to the official curation in our selected playlist: ▶ Playlist on YouTube.

Privacy & Multiplatform Architecture

Built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform for Android, Windows, and Linux. Local storage is secured using Room Database with hardware-backed AES-256 encryption.

Daily quotes and capsule messages are loaded locally from built-in resources, ensuring the application operates 100% autonomously without internet access.

Help Center & FAQ

What is Recovery Mode?

If database corruption causes a startup crash loop, the app boots into a secure Recovery Mode. From here, you can export an encrypted diagnostic log (.hldiag) or clear the cache to restore stability.

How do backups (.lifelogs) work?

Backups are exported in a secure .lifelogs package. Clicking these files in your device's file manager automatically triggers the import process in Hours Lived.

Exporting PDF Books (Light Novel)

You can format and export your stored memories into printed-style PDF books. The file is saved automatically under the Books/ subfolder inside your selected backup directory.

How does the desktop version work (Windows & Linux)?

Hours Lived Desktop is currently in Alpha. It provides full parity with the mobile version: 100% offline architecture, local AES-256 (SQLCipher) database encryption, cross-compatible backups (.lifelogs) between PC and phone, and Light Novel styled PDF export.
